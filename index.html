Angela Nelson

Owner & Financial Educator | McKinney, TX

Angela Nelson is the founder of Starship Solutions, a comprehensive financial services company dedicated to equipping, educating, and empowering families to achieve financial literacy and build lasting wealth. As a licensed WealthWave agent and LegalShield Associate, Angela brings expertise in financial education, life insurance, retirement planning, and legal protection services.

With a passion for helping families understand how money really works, Angela offers personalized financial education, one-on-one consultations, and group workshops throughout the Dallas-Fort Worth area and nationwide.
